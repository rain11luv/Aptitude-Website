Why we are building this

Every year, millions of students graduate without the essential communication and behavioral skills employers demand. Career centers are severely understaffed—often with one counselor for every 2,000 students. This creates an enormous gap between graduation and meaningful employment.

We founded Aptitude AI to democratize career preparation. By harnessing advanced AI and the OpenAI Realtime API, we offer a continuous memory-driven companion that provides personalized voice coaching and resumes tailored explicitly for you. We believe the first win in an interview should take less than 60 seconds of practice.
